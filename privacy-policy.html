OR Vault — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

OR Vault is an app for managing your valuable item collection. We take the protection of your data very seriously.

1. Data Controller

OR Vault — contact: support@objetrare.app
Distributed on the App Store (Apple Inc.) and Google Play Store.

2. Data Collected

  • Account: email address and password (secured via Supabase Auth).
  • Collection: item info (name, value, photos, documents) you voluntarily enter. This data belongs entirely to you.
  • Payment data: handled exclusively by Apple / Google Play / RevenueCat. We never store your banking information.
  • Anonymous analytics: if you consent, anonymized usage data helps us improve the app. No personal data is included.

3. Data NOT Collected

  • GPS location
  • Contacts or address book
  • Biometric data (Face ID / Touch ID — processed locally, never leaves your device)
  • Information about other installed apps

4. Data Security

  • Data stored on Supabase (AWS, EU region, GDPR compliant).
  • Photos and documents in a private bucket with row-level security. URLs are temporary (5 min) and signed.
  • All communications encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

5. Data Sharing

We never sell or share your data. Third-party processors:

  • Supabase — database and storage (GDPR, EU data).
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (anonymized user ID only).
  • OpenAI — OCR document extraction (images sent temporarily, not retained).

6. Data Retention

Data retained while your account is active. Deleting your account removes all data within 30 days.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Data portability
  • Opt out of sale of personal information (we do not sell data)

Contact: support@objetrare.app

8. Minors

OR Vault is intended for users 17+. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Contact

support@objetrare.app
